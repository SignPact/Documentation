# SignPact at a glance

SignPact replaces fragmented contract workflows with one platform that combines:

- AI drafting and review
- Built‑in e‑signature
- Contract lifecycle management (CLM)

Move from draft to signed deal faster, with clear risk visibility and simple governance.

## Core pillars

- e‑Signature: Secure, legally‑binding signatures with tokened links for recipients
- CLM: Organized repository, folders, permissions, version history, public view
- AI: Contract analysis and redlining, clause risk insights, summaries

## What you can do

1. Upload a document and place signature/form fields
2. Assign signers and send for signature (token links supported)
3. Review with AI redlining, address risks, and finalize
4. Track status and history in the Dashboard
