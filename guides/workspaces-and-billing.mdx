# Workspaces & billing

Admins manage seats and billing in Settings → Billing.

## Seats and members

- Add emails to invite members; update seat count accordingly
- Remove a member or leave the workspace

## Stripe portal and checkout

- Open billing portal to manage payment methods and invoices
- Create a checkout session to start a subscription with N seats

Notes

- Some routes (e.g., `/new-document`, `/generation`) are payment‑gated
