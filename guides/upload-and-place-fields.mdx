# Upload & place fields

Use `/new-document` to upload and prepare a document for signing.

## Steps

1. Upload a PDF/DOC/DOCX
2. Drag signature and form fields onto pages
3. Assign each field to a contact (signer)
4. Complete to create a signature request

Notes

- This route is payment‑gated
- Dev builds may show a method selection step (Generate preview is a stub)
