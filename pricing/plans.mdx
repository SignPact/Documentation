# Custom Pricing

SignPact offers bespoke pricing based on your workflow (e‑signature, CLM, AI), team size, and contract volume. We do not publish rigid tiers.

## How to evaluate

- Explore the Workflow Replacement demo at `/workflow-demo`
- Estimate savings using the ROI Calculator at `/roi-calculator`

## Typical packages (positioning)

- Launch Pack: Fast start with built‑in e‑signatures and AI summaries
- Pro Vertical Pack: Industry‑tuned redlining and one key integration
- Scale: Governance, multi‑workspace, audit logs, SSO

Note: Final pricing is tailored during discovery to your goals and compliance needs.
