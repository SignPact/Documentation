# Billing & workspaces

Billing is seat‑based and managed by workspace admins in Settings → Billing.

## Roles

- Admin: manages seats, billing, and invites
- Member: participates under the workspace subscription

## Actions

- Update seats, invite members, remove member, leave workspace
- Open Stripe billing portal, create checkout sessions
